⚙️ How The IoT Pipeline Works

1
Pulse Ingestion:

Light pulses (laser/LDR) or push-buttons are sampled by the microcontroller GPIO interrupts.

2
Edge Thresholding:

Onboard timing algorithms classify pulses into dots (1 unit) and dashes (3 units).

3
Decoded Payload Delivery:

Parsed text is rendered on the onboard OLED display and published via MQTT to the web dashboard.

🧰 Hardware Specifications

  • 🔹 MCU: ESP8266 NodeMCU or ESP32-WROOM
  • 🔹 Sensors: Light Dependent Resistor (LDR) / Laser Detector
  • 🔹 Display: 0.96" SSD1306 I2C OLED Display
  • 🔹 Indicators: Active Piezo Buzzer & 5mm High-Bright LED
  • 🔹 Connectivity: 2.4 GHz 802.11 b/g/n Wi-Fi
